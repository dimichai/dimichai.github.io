A bit about me

I was previously a PhD Candidate at the Civic AI Lab.

My research uses multi-agent systems and machine learning to study societal problems: how the behavior of individual agents produces collective outcomes, and how those outcomes are distributed across social groups.

In my PhD I developed (multi-objective) reinforcement learning methods for designing equitable public transport networks, and agent-based models of school segregation and inequality in congestion games.

Now I study generative social simulation with LLM-based agents: how agent design choices shape collective outcomes such as polarization and network segregation, and how to make these simulations more reliable for social science and policy.

I also work on prosocial recommender systems: who-to-follow recommendations designed to reduce echo chambers rather than maximize engagement, which I study through user experiments with real Bluesky users.

Outside academia, I am the co-founder of Greece in Figures, a website that explains complex socioeconomic phenomena to a Greek audience.

If any of this sounds interesting, write to me.
